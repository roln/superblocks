1. Privacy Policy

Star Chart Blocks is designed as a standalone, offline puzzle game. We value and respect your privacy:

  • Zero Data Collection: We do NOT collect, transmit, distribute, track, or store any personal data, user identifiers, device IDs, or precise location data.
  • No Account Required: The game does not require user registration, email sign-in, or third-party logins.
  • Local Storage Only: All progress, levels unlocked, and player settings are stored strictly on your local device via standard iOS sandboxed local storage.
  • No Analytics or Ads: The application does not integrate third-party tracking frameworks, analytics services, or advertising SDKs.
  • Children's Privacy (COPPA): Because we collect no personal data whatsoever, our application fully complies with COPPA and global child privacy regulations.
